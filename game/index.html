            About the brain signals

            Every tile is a real EEG recording from the PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery study: volunteers wearing a 64-electrode cap were cued to imagine moving their left hand, right hand, both hands or feet, or to rest.

            When you drop a tile, the simulator decodes that recording live (CSP + LDA on 21 channels over the motor cortex) and the robot acts on what it decoded. Easy and Normal deal recordings the decoder reads correctly; Real decoder deals any recording, so misreads happen, just like a real brain-computer interface.
